About Our Nursing Program

Our nursing program is designed to prepare students for a rewarding career in healthcare. We offer a comprehensive curriculum that combines theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical experience.

Program Highlights

  • State-of-the-art simulation labs
  • Experienced faculty with diverse nursing backgrounds
  • Clinical rotations at top healthcare facilities
  • High NCLEX pass rates
  • Career placement assistance

Admission Requirements

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Minimum GPA of 3.0
  • Completion of prerequisite courses
  • Personal statement
  • Letters of recommendation

Application Process

  1. Submit your application through our online form
  2. Provide all required documents
  3. Complete an interview (if selected)
  4. Await admission decision

For more information about our nursing program, please contact our admissions office.
